Estimated grid electricity saved = scenario solar-to-load + battery-to-load across the complete 24-hour plan. Percentage = estimated grid kWh saved ÷ ML-forecast demand kWh × 100. Compare with all-grid supply of the same demand; total building energy need does not decrease. Grid outages or unmet demand are not counted as savings.

Estimated grid purchase cost saved = estimated grid kWh saved × configured RM/kWh. The optional KPI tariff overrides valuation only; otherwise this scenario's RM/MYR price is used. Other currencies are not converted. User assumptions are saved in this browser; clear a field to remove it. No applicable tariff or official emissions factor is assumed. Costs exclude initial battery charging, degradation, CAPEX and tariff schedules. Carbon is calculated only with an entered factor, credits direct solar-to-load only, and excludes battery discharge because its charging source is unverified. Previews are cached for up to 15 minutes per building and input set; Refresh requests a new calculation. Approved plans retain their captured results.

Scenario estimate — no live control. ML predicts demand; dispatch and estimated grid-purchase savings are calculated from scenario assumptions. No savings probability, confidence guarantee or verified savings is available. Historical benchmark demand and demo weather have independent timelines.

Accuracy improvement = baseline model test MAE − forecast model test MAE; percentage uses baseline MAE on the same test sample count. These are evaluation results, not savings.
